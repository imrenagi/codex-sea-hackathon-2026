public-road chain through Medan Merdeka Barat, Selatan, Timur and Utara, using

the two rendered traffic lanes are a gameplay approximation. Cross streets are scenery.

ACCURACY
Footprints are mapped; known heights are retained and remaining heights estimated.
Monas building:part outlines at the monument are replaced by the authored landmark,
avoiding duplicate structures. Facades, vegetation, benches, lamps, the HI transit
entrance, pool details, pedestrians and smaller dimensions are approximations.
These are separate exploration areas, not one continuous road to Sopo Del.
The playable road loops have visible curbs; background streets and gardens are scenery.
The Monas park intentionally contains open lawns and landscaped space.
No Google imagery or extracted Google geometry is included. The game is offline.
