ACCURACY
The address, main road centerline, 7 m road-width tag, two-way designation and mapped
footprints are sourced. Tower A's 167 m height comes from OSM. Tower B uses an estimated
116 m height and the developer's 28-floor layout instead of OSM's conflicting level tag.
Other heights are mapped when present, otherwise estimates. Trees, markings, lighting,
plants, paving, canopy dimensions, plaza rings and smaller facade details are approximate.
The scene does not reproduce interiors or every current tenant. Sea is identified as
the destination in the game UI; giant Sea logos have not been invented on the real facade.
The playable vehicle corridor remains deliberately constrained to the main street.

ENVIRONMENT DRESSING — added for the game at the user's request
Unmapped gaps are filled with authored low- and mid-rise buildings, plain shop signs,
ground vegetation, shrubs, extra trees, benches, bins and service fixtures. These are
deliberate visual approximations, not newly surveyed buildings or businesses. Placement
reserves mapped footprints, streets and the Sopo Del forecourt. No source footprints move.
