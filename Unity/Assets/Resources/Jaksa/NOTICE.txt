ACCURACY
Road alignment and footprints: OSM extract. Heights: mapped building:levels where present,
otherwise estimates (including hotel massing). Road width 6.5 m and sidewalks 1.6 m
are gameplay/art estimates; the older OSM width tag is 3 m and has not been field-verified.
Façades, roofs, signs, paint, tree locations, furniture, and overhead wires are approximations.
Five additional blocks beyond the south junction are authored background scenery, not OSM footprints.
This is a playable environment prototype, not a survey-grade or current-condition replica.
